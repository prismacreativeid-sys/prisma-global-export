Frequently asked questions

Before we work together.

Every product and destination has different commercial and regulatory requirements.

Do you offer fixed minimum order quantities (MOQ)?

MOQs depend on the product, supplier and packaging requirements. Please share your desired volume so we can check feasibility.

Can you provide technical specifications and certificates?

Share the standards and documents required by your market. We will confirm what documentation is available for the specific product and supplier. Nothing is assumed to be certified.

Can you quote FOB, CFR or CIF?

Trade terms can be discussed after checking product availability, logistics, destination and the responsibilities of each party.

Can I request a sample before ordering?

You can request a sample in your inquiry. Sample availability, costs and international shipping feasibility are confirmed on a case-by-case basis.
