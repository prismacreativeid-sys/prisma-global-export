Privacy Notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Prisma Global Export is an international B2B trade-facing brand operated by PT Prisma Creative Indonesia. This website provides information for prospective business partners. We aim to handle business enquiries responsibly.

What information is requested

When submitting a quotation request, you provide your name, company, business email address, product, destination, intended quantity, preferred delivery term, and message. An optional direct conversation can take place through WhatsApp. Please do not submit sensitive financial details or identification documents through the form.

How enquiries are sent

When this website is hosted on Netlify with form detection enabled, your quotation request is transmitted to and stored by Netlify Forms, a third-party form processing service used for enquiry management. Company representatives with authorised Netlify account access can view the messages. An email alert may also be sent to the business email configured in Netlify notifications. If you use the WhatsApp link instead, WhatsApp processes that communication according to its own privacy terms. Forms opened locally from downloaded files are previews and are not submitted.

How we use received information

We use voluntarily submitted business enquiries to understand product requirements, respond to potential purchases, assess feasibility, and maintain relevant business correspondence. We do not claim that an enquiry constitutes an order or purchase agreement.

Data sharing and retention

We may share the relevant product requirements with prospective suppliers and logistics partners as needed to assess a business request. We do not sell buyer contact details. Enquiry records are retained as reasonably necessary for business correspondence and legal obligations; where possible, outdated records are periodically reviewed and deleted. Netlify retains submissions according to the site's configuration and applicable service terms.

Cookies and analytics

The downloadable version of this website does not include third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or an account system. Hosting providers may maintain standard technical access logs.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to your inquiry, contact the website operator, PT Prisma Creative Indonesia, by WhatsApp at +62 851 2219 2100 or by email at prismacreativeid@gmail.com.
